# Night Flix Privacy Notice (Closed Test)

Version: Test 2026-09-06. This notice covers the current invited test only. The effective time is shown in the publication record.

## 1. Responsibility and contact

The current test operator manages its users and content. Headquarters technical staff provide Night Flix maintenance and security support. Contact the person who supplied your installation link through the original invitation channel for privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests. The actual operating entity and direct privacy contact must be added before public launch; this test notice does not replace that work.

## 2. Information processed

- Account data: email address, username, hashed password and email verification status, to register, sign in and recover access. We do not need your QQ account login password.
- Verification and security data: code-verification records, delivery status, IP address, session, device/client information and timestamps, to send codes, prevent abuse and troubleshoot.
- Usage data: progress, history, favorites, likes, comments, messages, share events and test-credit or entitlement records when you use the corresponding features. Public comments and nicknames may be visible to other participants.
- Consent records: the document identifiers, versions and time you accept at registration.

Records arise from actual requests; this list does not imply every feature is enabled. Do not submit sensitive information unrelated to testing.

## 3. Purposes, providers and access

We use data for test functionality, security email, account maintenance, content moderation and troubleshooting. Registration consent is not authorization for marketing campaigns or sale of personal information.

The test runs on an Alibaba Cloud server in mainland China, with a database and backups used to operate and recover Night Flix. Verification emails currently use Tencent QQ Mail SMTP, which processes the recipient address, email text including the code, and delivery information. Your receiving email provider also processes the message. If you participate from another country or region, your submitted data is transmitted to this mainland-China test environment. Do not submit personal data if you do not accept this arrangement.

Real payment, live advertising, Google/Apple sign-in and push services are not enabled. This consent does not authorize arbitrary future purposes or providers; enabling them requires updated information and any applicable notices, consent and other requirements.

Operators' accounts and business data are tenant-isolated. Authorized operator personnel can access necessary data for their operations; authorized headquarters personnel may access information needed for maintenance, security and management. Tenant isolation does not mean headquarters can never access data. Registering does not automatically merge this test account into Shanchuang's creation-service accounts.

## 4. Protection and retention

Requests use HTTPS and passwords are hashed. Mail-provider credentials and short-lived delivery payloads are encrypted, and code verification uses hashes. Successful delivery clears the short-lived queue payload, but does not delete copies held by mail providers or recipients. No system guarantees absolute security; do not share accounts or codes.

Account and usage data are retained while needed for the test. Account deletion processes removable data; security audit, necessary entitlement/transaction and consent records may remain for their applicable purposes and configured retention. Processing results should explain retained categories and reasons. Deletion is not a promise to erase every record immediately. Backups and third-party mail copies require separate handling. Data no longer needed should be reviewed and cleared when testing ends.

## 5. Choices and requests

You may decline registration, stop using the service, or ask your inviter for access, correction, export or deletion. Identity checks may be required to protect your account, but do not send passwords or verification codes in chat. Deletion may remove access to test benefits; completing a request does not establish deletion of third-party email copies.

This test is limited to invited adults aged 18 or older. Report a minor's data or other inappropriate collection to the test organizer.

## 6. Updates

This notice describes the current test, not a production compliance determination. Material changes to purposes, recipients or rights require further notice and renewed consent where applicable. Chinese and English versions are test texts. If you do not understand the displayed language, ask your inviter before accepting.
